## Brief Description

You need to form a group of 3 people to work on a self-proposed project.
The goal of the project is to investigate the impact of data preprocessing, in particular, feature engineering and data integration, by finding and solving problems based on real world datasets.

Recall the data analytics processes:

- Defining the problem
- Collecting data from various sources
- Cleaning and processing data
- Exploring and analyzing data
- Interpreting the results and finding insights

For example,

You can do a classification task on one or more specific and related datasets. You explore the data to perform appropriate data pre-processing and investigate how the data pre-processing enhances the classification task.
The project problem / goal is open to any topics relevant to data analytics.

Here are two online data-set repositories for your reference:

- Awesome Public Datasets: https://github.com/awesomedata/awesome-public-datasets
- UCI Machine Learning Repository: http://archive.ics.uci.edu/

## Project Deliverables

Your project is worth 20% of your final grade, and the following shows the 3 deliverables.

### 1. Proposal Presentation (In-lecture Presentation on Oct 21, 2026)

You need to present your project topic and the plan for the project. The presentation should be around 10 minutes.
The presentation can include the following information:

- Project title
- Data set(s)
- Problem(s) that you try to address
- State why you find the problem(s) important or interesting
- A preliminary plan on the methodology

### 2. Final presentation (Due Date: Nov 25, 2026)

You need to give a 15-minute presentation to summarizes what you have done for your project and anything that would not be demonstrated in the written report.

Every member of your group should be present and present (introduce or demonstrate a part of your project) unless under special circumstances.

### 3. Final Report (Due Date: Nov 30, 2026)

You are recommended to include the following sections:

- Project title, student name and ID, email contact.
- Introduction:
  background, problem definition, related work.
- Design and Implementation:
  Overview of your solution, the insight of your solution (e.g. preprocessing, feature engineering, model (including the data mining/machine learning models) etc.), and implementation details.
- Evaluation:
  Experimental setup, performance metrics, experimental results.
  Most importantly, detailed analysis and discussion of the results, particular the comparison with the baselines.
- Conclusion:
  e.g. main finding(s) of your project, lessons learned, thoughts and suggestions for follow-up work.
- References

Here are some samples of the final report: Sample 1, Sample 2 (links on the Canvas page).

Formatting Your Report:

- Language: ALL MANUSCRIPTS MUST BE IN ENGLISH.
- Length: Must not exceed 10 pages, single-spaced and double-columned.
- File Format: All files must be submitted in one of the following formats: Microsoft Word or PDF.
- Formatting Your Paper. All text must be in a two-column, single-spaced format.
  - Please use the Manuscript Templates: https://www.ieee.org/conferences/publishing/templates.html
- References and Acknowledgements: Your paper should contain References and Acknowledgements. The acknowledgements part should describe the specific contribution of each group member in this project.

Note: For all projects, we may contact you for your code.
(Only the report will be graded, code will not be graded)
